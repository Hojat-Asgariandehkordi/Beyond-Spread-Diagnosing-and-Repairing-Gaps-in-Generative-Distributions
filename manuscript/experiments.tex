\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}
A few terms recur. An \emph{arm} is one generator configuration in a
study, and a \emph{panel} is an independent set of generated images
drawn with its own noise seeds. A \emph{probe} is a sampler used only to
measure a checkpoint, and a \emph{resolved probe} is a longer sampler that
passes its fidelity gates. A \emph{gate} is a pass/fail/unresolved check
against a declared margin, such as the support gate of
Sec.~\ref{sec:support} or a precision, alignment, or preference floor.
A comparison is \emph{frozen} when it is fixed before outcome samples are
drawn, and a decision is \emph{unresolved} when its interval overlaps the
margin.

\subsection{Datasets}
\label{sec:datasets}
We validate the audit's resolution on controlled deletions from Gaussian
mixtures and from cached image features, where the removed region and
its mass are known. The image experiments use four settings.
CIFAR-10 uses a public pretrained DDPM~\cite{krizhevsky2009cifar,ho2020ddpm}.
A 20,000-image DDIM100 teacher supplies 5,000/5,000/10,000 disjoint
fitting/calibration/evaluation images. CIFAR-10.1v6~\cite{recht2018cifar10}
provides a second, separately collected real reference of 2,000 images,
and an independently pretrained variance-preserving EDM
model~\cite{karras2022edm} provides a second checkpoint for solver
comparisons. For CIFAR-10 semantic regions, classifier consensus defines ten
accepted classes plus rejection; real-image consensus acceptance is
90.75\%, with 98.75\% accuracy among accepted images.

In MNIST~\cite{lecun1998mnist}, withholding digit~3 from training creates
a known data-relative deficit. Two calibrated classifiers define consensus
digit regions and rejection; accepted real-image accuracy is 99.916\%, and
rejection remains in every probability denominator. For text-to-image
generation, we compare SD1.5, native LCM-LoRA, one-step DMD2, and Sana-600M
\cite{rombach2022ldm,luo2023lcmlora,yin2024dmd2,xie2025sana} on 128 held-out
PartiPrompts~\cite{yu2022parti}. Disjoint pools supply 32 representation-fit,
256 calibration, and 48 development prompts. References are DDIM50 at
classifier-free guidance (CFG) 7.5 and Flow-DPM++50; DMD2 also uses its
training-matched CFG1.75 teacher. These are generator-relative
measurements; prompts supply no real-image coverage target.

\subsection{Experimental settings}
\label{sec:settings}
Diffusion samplers evaluate the denoiser on a grid of timesteps. Leading
spacing places the grid at multiples of the schedule length divided by the
number of calls, so the two-call grid starts at $t_0=500$; trailing
spacing starts at the final timestep. On CIFAR-10, the original study
compared 22 sampler and initialization configurations (arms) at two, four,
and eight calls, using a 2,048-image pilot and 10,000 fresh images per arm. An ablation on its paired seeds motivated a separate
six-arm confirmation (the arms of Fig.~\ref{fig:cifar_initializer}a) on
five fresh paired panels of 2,000 images from the
same checkpoint. Uncertainty is over generated panels and conditions on
the fixed real-data reference (Supp.~Sec.~F.3). At four and eight calls,
matched-grid controls hold trailing timesteps fixed, and covariance-aware
sampling is charged for both forward and Jacobian--vector-product calls.
All eight EDM arms share 2,048 noise inputs; Heun uses $2K-1$ calls for
$K$ integration steps.

The original MNIST comparison spans three training seeds, five training
states, and eleven samplers (165 evaluations); its paired restoration
contrast uses the frozen three-comparison adjustment. The follow-up
compares 8,000 ordinary-data and 8,000 continued-targeting updates from
identical model, optimizer, EMA, and random states. Each arm receives
16,000 diagnosis and 2,000 independent outcome samples. The crossed
probes combine two short samplers and three resolved probes, longer
samplers that pass their fidelity gates, with five MNIST
training states and three seeds (Supp.~Secs.~H.3--H.4). Each short sampler
is compared with a 25-call reference from the same checkpoint, and a
training test also requires the same real-data-relative deficit to persist
under all three resolved probes, each passing its fidelity gates. The
recommendations were frozen before outcome generation. The CIFAR-10
semantic check uses one adaptation seed and 3,000 matched updates.

For text-to-image generation, each prompt receives eight diagnosis and 16
separate outcome seeds. The complete matrix retains 29 SD and seven Sana
configurations, including step and guidance changes, stochastic sampling,
conditioning noise, and first-base-step LCM. Native schedules and
checkpoint conversions are checked against released implementations.
Prompt-level resampling keeps each prompt's candidate/reference pairing
intact and treats its seeds as within-prompt observations.

\subsection{Baselines}
\label{sec:baselines}
On CIFAR-10, the covariance start is compared with the standard start
under leading and trailing DDIM grids, and with DPM++ and UniPC at the
same call budget~\cite{song2021ddim,lu2022dpmpp,zhao2023unipc}. Two
alternative interventions isolate where the change acts: full-moment
initialization, which also matches the mean, and output covariance
restoration, which restores the covariance of the generated images.
We also test covariance-aware sampling~\cite{schioppa2026covariance}. On the EDM checkpoint we compare Euler and Heun integration.
On MNIST, ordinary continuation (training on the full data) is compared
with continued targeting (keeping the restored digit's doubled training weight)
from the same parent, with the full-data model as the fidelity control;
the CIFAR-10 semantic check adds event-ratio weighting. For regional
detection, we compare the depletion certificate with the
number-of-statistically-different-bins (NDB) test~\cite{richardson2018ndb}
and two projected rules. For sampler selection, we compare with leading
DDIM, Vendi, and teacher-feature Fr\'echet selection~\cite{heusel2017gans} on CIFAR-10, and with
quality plus Vendi, fixed Sana DPM++8, a 20-call fallback, and
CADS~\cite{sadat2024cads} in text-to-image generation. We do not use
precision, recall, or Vendi as alternatives to the certificate, because
they do not return a regional effect-size decision; they serve as fidelity
and selection controls instead.

\subsection{Evaluation metrics}
\label{sec:metrics}
KID compares distributions in Inception feature
space~\cite{binkowski2018demystifying}, and we report it $\times10^3$ in
figures. DINO coverage is the fraction of real reference neighborhoods
reached by generated features~\cite{naeem2020density}, and precision is
its fidelity counterpart. Class-mass error averages the absolute
real-to-generated probability differences across the ten accepted digit
regions, and total variation is half the sum of the eleven absolute gaps
when rejection is included. Spread is the trace ratio of
\cref{eq:spread}. In text-to-image generation, PickScore~\cite{kirstain2023pickapic}
and ImageReward~\cite{xu2023imagereward} measure learned preference. A frequency-range check
counts images within the real-training 1--99\% frequency range
(Supp.~Sec.~G.1).

We write a paired difference as $\Delta\,[\ell,u]$, where $\Delta$ is the
mean difference between repair and control and $[\ell,u]$ its interval.
CIFAR-10 intervals are Student-$t$ over five generated panels, with
nominal 95\% simultaneous coverage across the five prespecified contrasts
of the fresh confirmation under the panel-effect approximation; the
CIFAR-10.1 re-evaluation uses its own family of four contrasts. MNIST intervals are 97.5\% paired
Student-$t$ intervals over three training seeds, one for each of two
prespecified outcomes. Intervals labelled ordinary or descriptive are
unadjusted paired 95\% intervals. Text-to-image intervals resample whole
prompts, and detection rates use exact binomial intervals. All intervals
condition on the fixed evaluation references. Proposition~1 and the
support gate are exact finite-sample guarantees. The spread intervals and
the Student-$t$ intervals over panels or seeds are approximate, and they
capture sampling variability for a fixed checkpoint or training recipe,
not variation across independently pretrained models.
